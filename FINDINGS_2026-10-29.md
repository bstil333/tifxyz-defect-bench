# Vesuvius entry — the self-proximity fix, real-segment results, and a correction (2026-10-29)

Follows `GOAL_2026-10-29.md` (changes 1–3). Everything below was measured today; figures come from `runs/bench_20261029_183252` (original 2 segments), `runs/bench_14seg_20261029` (14 segments) and `runs/real_unlabeled_flags.json` (22 real segments).

## 1. A correction to my 2026-10-28 finding 3

**Finding 3 said** tifxyz-repair's `self_proximity` cue flags 8.3% of valid cells on *clean* surfaces, so its recall is luck. **The flag count is right; the explanation was incomplete.** I measured where the flagged pairs sit: on both PHerc0332 segments the pairs lie a nearly constant **411–422 columns apart** (one wrap of the scroll, about 8,400 voxels) and about 6–10 voxels apart in 3D, spread across the whole surface. These two "clean" segments are **multi-wrap bands**: each cell sits next to the neighboring turn of the same sheet. The tool's own README says self-proximity is ordinary winding clearance on long band surfaces and keeps those separate. So the cue fired because I fed it a surface type it documents as expected to fire on, not because it is broken on single-wrap patches. The "false alarm" label was mine, and "clean" meant "no planted defect", not "no self-proximity".

## 2. The fix

`tifxyz-repair`, branch `winding-aware-self-proximity` (local commit `6f6d01f`; patch at `patches/0001-winding-aware-self-proximity.patch`; 40 of 40 tests pass, including a new one).

* New option `inspect_patch(..., winding_aware=True)` and CLI `inspect --winding-aware`. **Default behavior is unchanged.**
* The rule: group grid-distant, 3D-close pairs by their grid offset (log-scaled 5% bins, sign removed, each bin pooled with its 26 neighbors). A group that holds at least **0.5% pairs per valid cell** is winding clearance and is dropped. A local sheet switch is small, and a mirrored fold has offsets that change from pair to pair, so both stay flagged (the new test checks a two-wrap band is silenced and a fold is not).
* **The threshold is a free parameter and I iterated on it using these segments.** Sensitivity on the original two segments: 0.25%, 0.5% and 1% give identical results; 2% leaves 8.2 clean flags per 1,000 and 5% leaves 30. The default sits in the middle of that plateau. It should be validated on more multi-wrap segments than we have (we have three).

## 3. Before and after

### The original two segments (PHerc0332, bench run 2026-10-29)

| | clean-surface flags per 1,000 cells | chance a flag lands in a defect-sized neighborhood | sheet jumps found | stretches found |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tifxyz-repair, default | 83.21 | 100% | 92% | 96% |
| tifxyz-repair, winding-aware | **0.12** | **5%** | **53%** | 78% |

Sheet-jump recall by offset, winding-aware: 0 of 60 at 5 voxels, 11 of 60 at 10, **57 of 60 at 20, 60 of 60 at 40**. Holes and normal flips stay at 240 of 240 because other cues find them. Before the fix the cue's recall could not be told from chance (a flag was near any defect anyway); after it, **it is evidence of detection**, and it finds sheet jumps of 20 voxels or more at 95–100% with 0.12 false flags per 1,000 cells. tifxyz-doctor found 5% and windcheck 67% at 20 voxels on the same cases.

### 22 real public segments, unlabeled (`runs/real_unlabeled_flags.json`)

Source: the Vesuvius Challenge open-data bucket (`vesuvius-challenge-open-data`, 22 tifxyz grids from 11 scrolls, 86 MB, list in `data_real/SOURCES.json`). There is no answer key; each figure is flags per 1,000 valid cells, including any real defects.

* **The option changes the output on only 3 of the 22 segments, and all three are multi-wrap bands.** PHercParis4 w073–076 (four wraps): **905.22 → 0.04**. PHercParis4 w126–127: **709.14 → 59.12**. PHerc0814 auto-grown (second segment): 41.59 → 26.72. **The other 19 are identical before and after**, so it does not touch single-wrap surfaces. (The two PHerc0332 segments from section 3 are separate from these 22; they went 107 → 0.10 and 60 → 0.15 per 1,000 cells.)
* **tifxyz-doctor and windcheck do not change** (they have no such cue); on Paris4 w073–076 windcheck flags 18.37 per 1,000, which I have not explained.

### Planted defects on 14 segments (the 2 original + 12 real ones with at least 50,000 cells), 560 cases per tool

| | clean-surface flags per 1,000 | sheet jumps ≥20 voxels | stretches ≥1.25× |
|---|---|---|---|
| tifxyz-repair, default | 29.78 | 383 of 420 and 420 of 420 | 100% |
| tifxyz-repair, winding-aware | 11.01 | 380 of 420 and 420 of 420 | 100% |

**This pooled result is the weaker one and I am reporting it as such.** The false-alarm density falls by 63% but is still 11 per 1,000, because the real segments carry their own flags (stretch regions, interior holes) that are not winding clearance, and the 14-segment "chance" column stays near 100%. These "clean" segments are not clean. So the 14-segment pool does not show the cue as clean evidence; **the 2-segment run does, and the per-segment table does** (`runs/bench_14seg_20261029/per_segment_clean.md`).

## 6. Validation on 24 more multi-wrap segments, and a threshold bug it found (added overnight, 2026-10-29)

**Data:** 24 high-resolution (7.91 µm) PHercParis4 range-named bands from the same public bucket (about 1.2–2.1 million cells each, 596 MB, list in `data_multiwrap/SOURCES.json`). Script: `validate_multiwrap.py`. Results: `runs/validation_multiwrap.json` (final) and `runs/validation_multiwrap.v1_no_stride.json` (first version).

**What it found.** My first winding rule required a coherent offset class to hold 0.5% pairs per valid point. On grids over 400,000 cells the tool samples every k-th point, which cuts the pair density per sampled point by k, so on 12 of these 24 bands the rule could never fire and flagged cells were left in place. **The threshold was not scale-free.** The fix scales the requirement by the sampling stride (`stride=1` on smaller grids, so every earlier number is unchanged).

| | default | first rule | stride-aware rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| mean self-proximity flags per 1,000 cells over the 24 bands | 2.10 | 0.84 | **0.14** |
| bands where the option changed nothing | n/a | 12 of 24 | 2 of 24 |

The two that still carry flags (0.77 and 0.61 per 1,000) are the widest bands (w010–027 and w028–037, 10 to 17 wraps), where the wrap period drifts too much along the surface to form one offset class. That is a stated limit, not a solved case.

**What this does not show.** These bands have no answer key, so the 0.14 is flags remaining, not verified false alarms removed; on the original two segments the same option still finds 95–100% of planted sheet jumps at 20 voxels and above.

**Re-run of the 14-segment planted-defect benchmark with the stride-aware rule** (`runs/bench_14seg_20261029_v2stride`): every figure in section 3 is unchanged (winding-aware clean flags 11.01 per 1,000 cells; sheet jumps at 20 voxels 380 of 420 and at 40 voxels 420 of 420), because the large bands that the stride fix affects are not in that set and the residual flags there come from real holes and stretched regions, not winding.
